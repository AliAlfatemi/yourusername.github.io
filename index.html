About Me

Hello! I'm Ali Alfatemi, a software engineer based in New York. I enjoy creating things that live on the internet. My interest in web development started back in 2018 when I decided to try editing custom Tumblr themes — turns out hacking together HTML & CSS is pretty fun!

Fast-forward to today, and I've had the privilege of working at an advertising agency, a start-up, and a huge corporation. My main focus these days is building accessible, inclusive products and digital experiences for a variety of clients.

When I'm not at the computer, I'm usually hanging out with my cat, reading, or running around the city.
